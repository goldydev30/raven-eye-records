The Arsenal

A musician's armour

A knight is known by his armour and a guitarist by their rig. The Arsenal catalogues the guitars, amps, pedals, drums and strings behind the roster: endorsements, signature models and what has been seen on stage.

Fan submissions

Know what they play?

Spotted a band's rig at a show, in a playthrough or in an interview? Send it in with a photo or a link as proof. Every submission is reviewed before it appears, and approved entries are marked Fan submitted.

Submissions are made through a short form on GitHub (a free account is needed). You can also email the details instead.
