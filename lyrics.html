How the codex works

Quoted with permission

Lyrics belong to the people who wrote them. Raven Eye publishes full lyrics only for artists on the label, and excerpts or translations for other bands only with their permission. For everything else, each artist page links to the band's lyrics archive on Encyclopaedia Metallum.

Bands: if you would like your lyrics, translations or the stories behind them in the codex, write to us.
