Demos

Submit your music

We listen to everything that is sent with care. Because this label is built around lyrics, a submission without words is only half a submission.

Send us

  1. A private streaming link (Bandcamp, SoundCloud or a cloud folder). Please no attachments.
  2. Your lyrics for every track, with translations if they are not in English, and a few lines on what each song is about.
  3. Band name, country, line-up and any previous releases or labels.
  4. Your Encyclopaedia Metallum page, if you have one.

Where

Email demos@raveneyerecords.com with the subject line Demo: Band name, Country.

We reply to every submission we are interested in. If you have not heard from us in eight weeks, the ravens have flown elsewhere for now.

Fans: submit gear

Know what a roster band plays? Add it to The Armory with a photo or link as proof. Every submission is reviewed before it appears.
