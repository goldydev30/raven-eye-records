About the label

The eye that watches the north

Raven Eye Records is a grassroots, independent label for black metal and its kin: pagan, folk, atmospheric and melodic extreme metal. In the old tales Odin's ravens, Huginn and Muninn, Thought and Memory, flew across the world each day and returned with what they had seen. That is our job too: find the music, remember it, and bring it back.

What we do

  • Promote. We champion a curated roster of bands from across the world, signed to us or not, and send listeners to buy direct from them and their labels.
  • Catalogue. The Lyrics Codex maps the themes running through the scene and keeps lyrics and commentary shared with permission.
  • Release. Small-run physical and digital releases for underground artists, with fair, transparent terms.

What we believe

The words matter. A riff carries you into the forest; the lyric tells you why you are there. We look for bands with something to say about myth, history, landscape, loss and the old ways.

We do not promote bands whose lyrics or members advance hatred against people for who they are.
